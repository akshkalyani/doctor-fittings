About Doctor Fittings

Leading the industry with precision manufacturing and unwavering commitment to excellence since 2001.

Our Story

Founded in 2001, Doctor Fittings began with a simple mission: to provide the highest quality pipe fittings and connectors to industrial and commercial customers worldwide.

Over the past two decades, we've grown from a small manufacturing operation to a leading provider of premium fittings, serving thousands of clients across multiple industries.

Our commitment to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction has been the cornerstone of our success. Every product that leaves our facility meets the strictest quality standards and is backed by our experienced technical support team.
