About Us

Ashik Enterprises —
Your Trusted Hardware Partner

Established in 2010, Ashik Enterprises is a well-recognized wholesale and import firm specializing in architectural hardware for windows and doors. We supply premium-quality products manufactured using high-grade materials sourced from reputed vendors.

Our products serve residential apartments, commercial offices, malls, and industrial buildings. We cater to builders, glaziers, contractors, and hardware retailers across India, with bulk supply capability and consistently competitive pricing.
